# 3) Pillar 1 — CQC: Clinical Quality & Continuity of Care

**Pre-Qualification Cues** (any of these is enough to attempt qualification):

* Mentions of diagnosis accuracy, treatment appropriateness, medication safety, infection prevention, handover quality, discharge clarity, follow-up adherence, telehealth clinical adequacy, delays with clinical risk.
* Signals of **care pathway breaks** between settings (primary ↔ secondary ↔ tertiary), missing lab/imaging follow-through, unclear escalation.

**Typologies / Sub-Dimensions**

* Diagnostics: missed/delayed diagnosis; inadequate triage.
* Therapeutics: wrong treatment/route/dose; monitoring gaps.
* Safety Events: near misses; device or medication errors; infection control.
* Handover & Discharge: incomplete info, reconciliation failures, unclear self-care.
* Monitoring & Follow-Up: gaps in chronic care; results not communicated.
* Clinical Governance Drift: deviation from guidelines; unreviewed variations.

**Severity Rubric (anchor examples)**

* 1: Isolated process hiccup with no safety consequence (e.g., minor discharge wording confusion).
* 2: Recurring ambiguity or localized delays with potential but limited risk.
* 3: Cross-team/cross-site patterns; near misses; repeated handover failures.
* 4: Safety-critical failure likelihood; harm plausible or reported; systemic breaches of clinical standard.

**Evidence Anchors**

* Patient/caregiver phrases like “wasn’t told how to…”, “meds changed without explanation”, “results never shared”.
* Journey points: admission/discharge/transition of care; “night shift handover”; “post-op day 1”.
* PES ≥3 supports impact.

**Mitigation Cues (examples)**

* Standardize discharge & follow-up templates with teach-back.
* Reconcile meds at every transition; close the loop on test results.
* Embed escalation criteria in triage; audit variation vs guidelines.

**Boundary Notes**

* If the ED is primarily about **consent/choice**, route to **HRA**.
* If the ED is about **public transparency/brand fallout**, consider **PRC**.
* If the ED is about **reach/affordability/geography**, consider **AHS**.

# 4) Pillar 2 — HRA: Health Rights & Autonomy

**Pre-Qualification Cues**

* Informed consent issues, poor risk/benefit communication, language/cultural barriers, dignity/privacy concerns, refusal not respected, access to records, second-opinion friction, safeguarding.

**Typologies / Sub-Dimensions**

* Consent Integrity: timing, comprehension, voluntariness, documentation.
* Information Rights: plain-language explanations, interpreter provision.
* Privacy & Dignity: sensitive exams; gender concordance requests.
* Choice & Control: shared decision-making; advance directives; refusals.
* Vulnerable Populations: minors, cognitively impaired, safeguarding concerns.

**Severity Rubric**

* 1: Polite but incomplete explanation; quickly remediable.
* 2: Recurrent weak consent language or missing interpreter support.
* 3: Systemic pattern undermining choice or privacy; complaints trending.
* 4: Coercion, non-consensual procedures, or safeguarding red flags.

**Evidence Anchors**

* Verbatims about “no one explained risks”, “felt pressured”, “no interpreter”.
* Interaction moments: consent signing, pre-op counseling, sensitive procedures.

**Mitigation Cues**

* Standardize consent scripts & timing; mandate teach-back.
* Guaranteed interpreter pathways; privacy protocols in high-risk settings.
* Embed shared-decision tools; visible opt-out/second-opinion options.

**Boundary Notes**

* If patient trust fallout is **public/community-facing**, add **PRC**.
* If the harm is **clinical process failure**, add or shift to **CQC**.
* Pure access/wait issues with no autonomy angle → **AHS**.

# 5) Pillar 3 — PRC: Public Reputation & Community Trust

**Pre-Qualification Cues**

* Media/social amplification, community backlash, patterns of complaints that erode trust, transparency after incidents, perceived unfairness in who gets advanced care.

**Typologies / Sub-Dimensions**

* Transparency & Candor: disclosures after adverse events.
* Complaint Handling & Apology Quality.
* Community Equity Signals: who gets access to innovations.
* Staff Culture & Empathy Perception.
* Misinformation Containment & Public Health Messaging.

**Severity Rubric**

* 1: Localized dissatisfaction; low visibility.
* 2: Repeated complaints in one service line; trust wobble.
* 3: Cross-service reputation dent; media attention likely.
* 4: Institution-wide credibility risk; regulator/stakeholder escalation.

**Evidence Anchors**

* Words like “cover-up”, “no apology”, “no updates”, “ignored complaints”.
* Journey anchors around incident follow-up, complaint response timelines.

**Mitigation Cues**

* Issue timely, plain-language updates; restorative apology pathways.
* Community roundtables; publish fairness metrics for access decisions.
* Rapid response playbooks for misinformation.

**Boundary Notes**

* If the core harm is clinical safety → **CQC** (PRC may be secondary).
* If trust erosion stems from autonomy violations → **HRA** linkage.
* If the root is access inequity → also flag **AHS**.

# 6) Pillar 4 — AHS: Access, Health Equity & System Sustainability

**Pre-Qualification Cues**

* Wait times, distance/transport barriers, affordability, digital exclusion, rural/low-resource settings, surge capacity (pandemics/disasters), workforce shortages, supply fragility.

**Typologies / Sub-Dimensions**

* Availability: hours, staffing, modality (in-person/telehealth/mobile).
* Affordability: out-of-pocket burden; catastrophic spend risk.
* Geographic Reach: rural/remote coverage; referral leakage.
* Inclusivity: disability access; language; digital divide.
* Resilience: emergency surge, backlog recovery, supply chain.

**Severity Rubric**

* 1: Mild delay/inconvenience; alternatives exist.
* 2: Recurring access friction for a subgroup or service line.
* 3: Multi-population inequity or backlog with worsening clinical risk.
* 4: Systemic, sustained inaccessibility; crisis-level resilience gap.

**Evidence Anchors**

* Phrases like “can’t afford”, “no slots”, “too far”, “app doesn’t work for…”
* Interaction moments: booking, triage, referral, follow-up scheduling.

**Mitigation Cues**

* Outreach clinics/mobile units; telehealth + interpreter support.
* Protected capacity for high-risk cohorts; transportation vouchers.
* Surge playbooks; cross-training; supplier diversification.

**Boundary Notes**

* If the consequence is clinical harm → co-flag **CQC**.
* If access is denied without transparent criteria → **HRA** link.
* Community fallout from inequity → **PRC** link.
