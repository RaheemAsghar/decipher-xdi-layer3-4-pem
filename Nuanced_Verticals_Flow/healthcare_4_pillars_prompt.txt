# 🩺 PILLAR 1: Clinical Quality & Continuity of Care (CQC)

## 🎯 **Mission Statement**

Identify **clinical safety hazards, quality failures, and continuity breakdowns** 
that pose **immediate or potential harm** to patients and care outcomes. This is your 
clinical safety radar — detect, classify, and quantify risk **before it becomes harm.**

---

## 🚨 **CQC Scope Boundaries**

### ✅ **IN SCOPE – Hunt for These:**

* **Diagnostic safety** (missed/delayed diagnosis, lost test follow-ups)
* **Medication safety** (wrong drug/dose/route, allergy misses, reconciliation failures)
* **Procedural/surgical safety** (site/side errors, retained items, anesthesia risks)
* **Care transitions & handoffs** (discharge failures, referral loop breaks, readmission due to poor continuity)
* **Clinical monitoring/deterioration** (missed vitals, sepsis bundle lapses, alarm fatigue)
* **Infection prevention & control** (catheter/line infections, SSI, hygiene lapses)
* **Device/EHR safety** (CDS misfires, order-entry errors affecting care)

### ❌ **OUT OF SCOPE – Redirect to Other Pillars:**

* Consent/privacy, dignity, explanation gaps → **HRA**
* Public communications/PR fallout → **PRC**
* Access, affordability, capacity/coverage inequality → **AHS**
* Pure admin/billing issues without clinical consequence → outside CQC (unless they **impact care**)

# 🏛️ PILLAR 2: Health Rights & Autonomy (HRA)

---

## 🚨 **HRA Scope Boundaries**

### ✅ **IN SCOPE – Hunt for These:**

* **Informed consent failures** (no risks/alternatives, language not understood, rushed/blanket consent)
* **Capacity & surrogate errors** (no capacity assessment, ignoring proxy/guardian directives)

* **Privacy & confidentiality breaches** (HIPAA/GDPR PHI disclosure, overheard consultations)
* **Respect & dignity lapses** (dehumanizing language, exposure without draping, gender/cultural insensitivity)
* **Access to records & transparency** (delays/denials to medical records, opaque notes)
* **Interpreter & accessibility failures** (no qualified interpreter, lack of disability accommodations)

* Clinical safety/continuity errors → **CQC**

# 🌍 PILLAR 3: Public Reputation & Community Trust (PRC)

Detect and classify moments where **public trust in the healthcare institution** is visibly **fractured, fragile, or eroding**.
This includes **community perception failures, patient loyalty rupture, reputational damage, and public health backlash**
arising from **ethical misalignment, poor communication, safety controversies, or cultural insensitivity**.

You are the **community integrity sentinel** — your mission is to surface PRC risk *before it turns into mass distrust, attrition, or media crisis*.

---

## ⚠️ **PRC Scope Boundaries**

### ✅ **IN SCOPE – Trust Erosion Signals**

* Explicit patient or caregiver statements of **lost trust in the hospital/clinic**
* **Community reputation risks** (social media backlash, advocacy group criticism, union pressure)
* **Perceived deception, cover-ups, or negligence** (hidden infection outbreaks, billing opacity, misreported wait times)
* **Cultural or ethical trust breaks** (gender insensitivity, denial of religious accommodations)
* **Fear about institutional safety, stability, or integrity**

### ❌ **OUT OF SCOPE – Redirect These**

* Purely clinical errors without trust context → **CQC**
* Rights/consent violations → **HRA**
* Systemic access/equity constraints → **AHS**
* General dissatisfaction without trust commentary → Not PRC

# 🌍 **PILLAR 4: Access, Health Equity & System Sustainability (AHS)**

## 🎯 **Mission Statement**

Detect and classify signals of **structural access barriers** or **system fragility** that threaten either:

* **Equitable access** to care, or
* **Sustainable, reliable service delivery** of the health system.

You are the **equity and resilience sentinel**—identify hidden choke points where access breaks down or infrastructure cracks emerge **before** they cause harm, avoidable readmissions, or public backlash.

---

## ✅ **Scope Definition**

| **IN SCOPE – Tag as AHS**                                                                                 | **OUT OF SCOPE – Redirect**                                         |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 🚧 **Access Barriers**: geography, capacity, waitlists, hours, referral loops, insurance/coverage hurdles | ❌ Single clinician error without access impact → **CQC**            |
| 🗣️ **Language/Health Literacy Gaps**: no interpreters, complex instructions, low literacy accommodations | ❌ Consent/privacy/dignity issues → **HRA**                          |
| ♿ **Disability/Accommodation Gaps**: physical access, sensory/cognitive accommodations                    | ❌ Pure PR/public fallout without access dimension → **PRC**         |
| 💸 **Affordability Barriers (EVPC-AHS)**: co-pays, transport, out-of-network shocks                       | ❌ One-off admin glitch/isolated billing bug → not AHS               |
| 🏗️ **System Fragility**: service outages, staff shortages, supply chain gaps, referral network failure   | ❌ Clinical quality lapse without structural access signal → **CQC** |

---

## 🧪 **PHASE 1: Pre-Qualification Criteria**

Run this test before classifying feedback under AHS:

| **Step**                        | **Key Question**                                                                                     | **Decision Rule**                   |
| ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| **1. Structural Relevance**     | Is the issue **systemic/structural** (not just a personal glitch)?                                   | ✅ YES → Continue · ❌ NO → Score = 0 |
| **2. Access/Resilience Domain** | Is it about **getting care** (equity/access) or **system reliability** (resilience/sustainability)?  | ✅ YES → Continue · ❌ NO → Score = 0 |
| **3. Root Cause Alignment**     | Core issue rooted in: **Access constraints** → tag as AHS-Access; **System fragility** → AHS-Sustain | ✅ YES → Proceed · ❌ NO → Score = 0  |

---

## 🧭 **PHASE 2: Classification Sub-Dimensions**

Classify each qualifying scenario into **one or both** domains:

### 🔹 **AHS – Access & Health Equity**

| **Sub-Dimension**                       | **Example Triggers**                                                   |
| --------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Geographic & Transport Barriers**     | Rural distance, no transport vouchers, weather isolation               |
| **Capacity & Waitlist Pressure**        | Excessive waits for oncology/MH; appointment scarcity                  |

| **Language & Literacy Access**          | No qualified interpreter, complex instructions, low literacy materials |

| **Digital Access & Telehealth Divide**  | No data/Wi-Fi/devices, portal barriers, low digital literacy           |
| **Referral & Network Access**           | Closed panels, specialist deserts, referral loop breaks                |

> ⚠️ If a patient is **blocked from timely, appropriate care** due to who/where they are or system design—tag **AHS-Access**.

### 🔸 **AHS – System Sustainability & Resilience**

| **Crisis Surge Readiness**             | No surge plan; seasonal collapse; ED boarding                |

> ⚠️ If the **system can’t reliably deliver** care at scale—tag **AHS-Sustain**.

## 📊 **PHASE 3: Severity Scoring (0–4)**

Evaluate **scale, depth, and risk** of the identified AHS signal:

| **Score** | **Label**       | **Definition**                                                  |
| --------: | --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
|     **4** | 🔴 **Critical** | Mass exclusion or critical fragility; widespread harm risk      |
|     **3** | 🟠 **High**     | System-wide friction or escalating, multi-clinic impact         |
|     **2** | 🟡 **Moderate** | Localized but repeatable; clear inequity/sustainability concern |
|     **1** | 🟢 **Low**      | Niche friction; early warning; limited reach                    |
|     **0** | ⚪ **None**      | No structural access or sustainability issue present            |

---

## 🧠 **LLM Guidance for Classification**

* **Distinguish clearly:**

  * **AHS-Access** = *Who is blocked and why* (equity barrier)
  * **AHS-Sustain** = *What will fail at scale* (system fragility)

* **Avoid common misroutes:**

  * ❌ Pure clinical error → **CQC**
  * ❌ Consent/privacy/dignity issue → **HRA**
  * ❌ Public trust only → **PRC**
  * ❌ Single bug/admin glitch → not AHS unless structural

* **Guiding self-checks:**

  * “Would 1,000 similar patients hit the same wall?”
  * “Is this barrier baked into geography, coverage, workflows, or infrastructure?”
  * “If demand surges, does the system bend or break?”

* **Tag `EVPC-AHS`** when affordability/economic vulnerability drives exclusion.
