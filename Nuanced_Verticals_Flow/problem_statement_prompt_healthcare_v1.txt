Generate a **diagnostic-grade, clinically faithful Problem Statement** to kick off the Plan phase of the PDCA lifecycle for healthcare organizations.

- Begin with **WHAT** (patient reality + matters), then layer **WHEN** and **WHERE**.

- **Scale Clinical Excellence** → Frame as proven best practice worthy of systematic replication
- **Innovate Care Model** → Frame as forward-looking capability that meets emerging patient/market expectations

### Patient Safety Override
If `action_axis` is "Restore Safety", the problem statement MUST emphasize:
- Patient harm risk or actual harm
- Duty of care breach
- Clinical error or diagnostic failure
- Access failure with health consequences
- Privacy/consent violation

Use urgent, direct language. This is non-negotiable.

## 🔄 HEALTHCARE THREE-AXIS FRAMEWORK ALIGNMENT

This prompt reflects the **Healthcare-Specific Universal Execution Contract** three-axis framework:

### INTENT_AXIS (Speech-Act in Healthcare Context)
- What the patient/caregiver is doing with language
- Present in `meta` object for context
- NOT required for problem statement construction
- Healthcare values: **Clinical Praise, Safety Concern, Care Coordination Request, Process Improvement Suggestion, Clinical Clarification Question, Patient Behavior Pattern, Healthcare Trend Signal**

### AFFECT_AXIS (Clinical Execution Pressure)
- Emotional pressure / urgency tied to care quality, safety, or access
- Present in `meta` object for context
- NOT required for problem statement construction
- Healthcare values: **Trust (+3), Reassurance (+1), Uncertainty (0), Anxiety (-1), Fear (-3)**
- These map to 5-tier pressure lattice for ERI computation

### ACTION_AXIS (Healthcare Organizational Response Class)
- **EXECUTION-CRITICAL** — the strategic response posture
- Determines organizational action: Restore Safety / Improve Care Delivery / Scale Clinical Excellence / Innovate Care Model
- MUST appear in problem statement and WHY logic
- Driven by `action_axis_justification` field

**Key Principle:**
The ACTION_AXIS is the only axis that directly shapes the problem statement's strategic framing. The other two axes provide clinical context and emotional fidelity but are not structural to the PDCA mission definition.

**Patient Safety Priority:**
Restore Safety signals receive elevated urgency and direct language. This is non-negotiable in healthcare.
